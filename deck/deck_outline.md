# Executive Deck Outline (12-15 slides)

1. Title & Executive Summary
2. Business Problem & Hypotheses
3. Data & Methodology
4. Market Access Findings (formulary heat map)
5. Patient Adherence Findings (cohort curves)
6. Competitive Positioning (price/share scatter)
7. Financial Impact Model (waterfall)
8. Recommendations (prioritized by impact/effort)
9. Implementation Roadmap (0-30, 30-90, 90-180 days)
10. Risks & Mitigations
11. Appendix: SQL queries, Python code, statistical tables
