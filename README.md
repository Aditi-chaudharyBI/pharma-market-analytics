# Pharma Market Analysis

**Pharmaceutical market intelligence & patient adherence analysis**  
*Business context modeled on PwC Healthcare Advisory engagements*

## 📋 Business Problem
A cardiovascular drug is losing market share (22% → 15% in 18 months). New patient starts are declining, and patient adherence is below benchmark. This project analyzes real-world prescription, payer, and outcomes data to identify root causes and recommend recovery actions.

## 🔍 Key Findings
| Finding | Impact |
|---------|--------|
| 68% of Medicare Part D plans require prior auth (vs 12% for competitor) | Blocks new patient starts |
| 42% of discontinuations occur in first 90 days | Driven by high copay (>$75/mo) |
| Competitor holds Tier 2 placement on 74% of Commercial plans | Price advantage |
